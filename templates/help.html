Quick start guide

Follow these steps in order. They match cards 1, 2 and 3 on the Home page.

Before you start

  • Connect the Pluto to the Jetson by USB and confirm that it is powered.
  • Install the latest PlutoDVB2 firmware by Evariste (F5OEO): download PlutoDVB2.
  • Connect the antenna, dish and power amplifier. Never transmit into an open RF connector.
  • Prepare a receiver so that you can monitor your signal, such as a MiniTiouner running the modified OpenTuner version for Jetson DATV.
  • Make sure that your amateur radio licence permits QO-100 operation, then enter your callsign on the Setup page.
1

Choose what to send

In card 1 on the Home page, select a source:

  • Testcard — sends a still image with audio. Use the ‹ › arrows to select a testcard. SBB Clock (live) displays a live station clock with test tones.
  • Camera — sends live video and audio from the selected USB camera and audio input.
  • Video — sends a prepared file from the Video Library.
Recommended for a first test: select a testcard; it is the easiest source to verify on a receiver.
2

Choose where to send it

Card 2 shows the live BATC wideband spectrum. Each bar below the spectrum represents an available channel position.

  • Green indicates a free channel. Red indicates activity.
  • Select a green bar. Its frequency appears in the Freq. field. Do not select an occupied channel.
  • SR is the symbol rate. 333 kS/s uses less bandwidth and is easier to receive; 500 kS/s supports better picture quality but requires a stronger signal.
  • FEC 3/4 provides more picture data; FEC 2/3 provides stronger error protection.
Recommended starting point: keep the default settings, SR 333 and FEC 3/4.
3

Go on air

  1. Keep TX power at 0% before starting.
  2. Press ▶ Start stream. After a few seconds the button turns red and says ■ Stop stream.
  3. Check the Local Pluto RX spectrum. A stable, flat-topped signal confirms that the Pluto is transmitting.
  4. If you use the optional PTT relay, wait until the signal looks stable, then press Switch PTT relay ON. The spectrum status is advisory; the operator controls the relay.
  5. Increase TX power gradually while watching your signal on the BATC spectrum.
  6. Check your receiver: you should see your picture and hear the sound.
  7. When you are done, press ■ Stop stream. TX power goes back to 0% and the PTT relay switches off automatically.
Keep your signal below the beacon level. The beacon is the large signal on the left of the BATC spectrum. Reduce TX power immediately if your signal reaches that level.

PTT relay (optional)

The optional relay can control the first stage of an external power amplifier, such as a driver or preamplifier.

Purpose: the Pluto can produce brief high-level output while starting or being reconfigured, regardless of the power setting. Keep the external amplifier disabled until the transmitted signal is stable.
  • The relay can be switched on only while a stream is running.
  • It switches off when you press Switch PTT relay OFF, stop the stream, or the transmission process ends.
  • Wiring: use physical pin 18 on the Jetson's 40-pin header and a ground pin, such as physical pin 20. On = 3.3 V; off = 0 V.
Use the physical pin number. Pin 18 does not mean “GPIO18.” Use a relay module with a 3.3 V logic input and a transistor or optocoupler. Never connect a bare relay coil to the Jetson GPIO pin.

Add your own videos

  1. Connect a USB drive to the Jetson and select it once on the Setup page.
  2. Open the Video Library, choose a source file, enter an output name, then select Upload & prepare video.
  3. Wait for the upload and conversion to finish. Preparation can take approximately as long as the video's running time.
  4. When the status reads Ready for DATV, reload the Home page if necessary. The file will be available under Video.
  5. Optional: select Copy to SD next to a video to keep it on the SD card, so it stays available without the USB drive. Copying needs the video's size plus 500 MiB free on the SD card.
While a video is being prepared, keep the USB drive connected and do not restart the Jetson or the Video Library service. An interruption may require the conversion to be retried.

Setup page

  • Callsign — updates the callsign used by the app and Pluto. Applying a change stops any active transmission and reboots the Pluto.
  • QRA locator — the locator shown on the SBB Clock testcard.
  • Top/bottom banner & marquee — configures the overlays used by Camera and Video sources. Testcards are unchanged.
  • OpenTuner — configures automatic tuning, LNB correction and TX frequency correction.
  • Add prepared videos via USB — selects the USB drive used by the Video Library.
  • Restart app — stops any active transmission and restarts the control-panel service. Allow about five seconds.

Receive with OpenTuner (optional)

A MiniTiouner and the modified OpenTuner application can send received video and receiver data to this web interface:

  • On the RX page, select a signal on the spectrum to tune OpenTuner. The received video and values such as margin, MER and MODCOD appear below.
  • While transmitting, card 1 on the Home page becomes an On-air monitor for your signal received through the satellite.

Setting it up

  1. Install the modified OpenTuner on a Windows PC connected to the same network as the Jetson.
  2. Under Extra Features, enable Quick Tune Control.
  3. Enable Jetson Stream. In its settings, enter the Jetson's IP address and port 5001.
  4. In the OpenTuner section of the Setup page, enable Auto-tune on stream start. Confirm the UDP port and LNB offset; the defaults are 6789 and 9750000 kHz.
  5. Select Save, then Test: tune to beacon. OpenTuner should tune to and lock on the wideband beacon.
The RX page opens on the beacon with audio muted. Use the player's speaker control to enable audio. During retuning, the last frame remains visible until the new stream is available.

Troubleshooting

“Select a frequency first”
Select a green channel below the BATC spectrum before starting the stream.
The BATC spectrum is empty or says DISCONNECTED
The spectrum requires internet access. Check the Jetson's network connection, then reload the page.
The RX spectrum still says NO SIGNAL after Start
Check the Pluto's USB connection. Stop the stream, then start it again.
“Checking spectrum…” does not change
The app has not detected a stable DVB-S2 spectrum. Inspect the local spectrum before deciding whether to operate the PTT relay.
The picture stutters on my receiver
Check the received signal margin and network connection. If they are stable, try another receiver program or player.
My video is not in the list
Check the preparation status in the Video Library and confirm that the selected USB drive is connected. Reload the Home page after preparation finishes.
The RX page or the On-air monitor says “No signal”
OpenTuner must be running and locked. Confirm that Jetson Stream is enabled with the correct Jetson IP address and port 5001.
OpenTuner does not react when I click a signal
Enable Quick Tune Control in OpenTuner. If broadcast tuning does not work, use Use this PC on the Setup page to address the PC directly, then save the settings.
A page looks wrong after an update
Reload the page. On most desktop browsers, press F5.

Operating practice on QO-100

  • Use only a free channel.
  • Keep your signal below the beacon level.
  • Keep test transmissions short.
  • Your callsign is shown in the picture — make sure it is set correctly on the Setup page.
